Validate the candidate list and split the back-test range into the walk-forward train/test windows.

Shared by the optimal selectors so that the two implementations cannot drift apart.

参数
querythe back-test query
dates[out] the trading calendar of the query
train_ranges[out] the [train_start, train_end) index pairs
返回
false when the calculation must stop (empty candidate list or a candidate bound to a security of its own)
