Fixed trade quantity money management strategy

A fixed quantity is bought every time.

参数
nthe quantity bought every time (it should be an integer multiple of the minimum trade quantity of the trading object, the program does not check this here)
注解
1) This strategy is mainly used to test and compare the results with the other strategies, it does not conform to the reality itself.
2) This strategy does not judge the existing positions; if a trade cannot be made with the existing positions, that judgment should be the responsibility of the System itself

Percentage risk model

See "Financial Freedom Through Electronic Day Trading" (June 2008, China Machine Press) by Van K. Tharp, P312 Formula: P (position size) = C (total risk) / R (risk per share) [here C, the cash, is the total risk]

参数
pthe percentage of the available cash risked on every trade, e.g. 0.02 means 2% of the available cash
