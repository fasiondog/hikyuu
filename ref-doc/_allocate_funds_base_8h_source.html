1/*
2 * AllocateFundsBase.h
3 *
4 * Copyright (c) 2025 hikyuu.org
5 *
6 * Base class of the portfolio-level fund allocation (AF), it is held by the aggregate form
7 * (MultiSystem) only.
8 *
9 * AF is composed of three replaceable algorithm parts, each of them corresponds to one virtual
10 * function (L1/L2/L3):
11 *
12 * - **L1 system-level allocation** `_allocate`: decide "how much each sub-system may manage".
13 * Input is the sub-system context list, output is the weight table; in mode B (Fund
14 * Allocation) it also writes the real cash quota into `contexts[i].quota` (weight x the parent
15 * net assets, or the fixed amount). Examples: AF_EqualWeight (1/N), AF_FixedWeight,
16 * AF_MultiFactor (weighted by the SE score).
17 *
18 * - **L2 behavior-level conversion** `_toTargets`: convert the sub-system suggestions into the
19 * executable quantity of the parent account (the folding point from the sub-system space to
20 * the individual instrument space). In mode A (Signal Aggregation) the target position market
21 * value = weight x net exposure ratio x the parent net assets, aggregated per instrument and
22 * rebalanced by the delta to the current position; in mode B the real instruction of the
23 * sub-system passes through unchanged.
24 *
25 * - **L3 portfolio risk control** `_checkRisk`: clip the suggestions at the portfolio
26 * dimension (e.g. the single instrument concentration cap `max_single_position`); mode B
27 * respects the sub-manager autonomy and skips the clipping.
28 *
29 * The overall entry is `allocate()` = L1 -> L2 -> L3. When the quota is needed before driving
30 * the sub-systems (the mode B calibration), use `allocateQuota()` (L1 only) and
31 * `allocateTargets()` (L2 -> L3) separately.
32 * The running mode ("A" Signal Aggregation / "B" Fund Allocation / "C" Shared Account
33 * Compatibility) is **owned by MultiSystem** and is passed explicitly into every L1/L2/L3 hook
34 * as the trailing `mode` argument; AF itself does not hold it. See the MultiSystem class comment
35 * for the three modes.
36 * The single-security form uses MoneyManagerBase (MM); the two no longer share the class
37 * hierarchy, the parameter family or the allocation mode.
38 * Created on: 2018-1-30
39 * Author: fasiondog
40 */
