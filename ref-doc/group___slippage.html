详细描述

The price indicated by a buy or sell signal generated by the system usually cannot be used to actually buy or sell at exactly that price in a real environment, due to the operation delay, the jump of the quoted price, and so on. The deviation between such a theoretical price and the price at which the buy may actually happen is called "slippage". In a system simulation the influence of the slippage should be fully considered, and it is also a test of the system stability, i.e. a small deviation does not have a large influence on the final return of the system.
