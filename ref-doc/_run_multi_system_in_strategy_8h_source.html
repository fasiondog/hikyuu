7 * The live trading entry of the aggregate system (MultiSystem).
8 * Run MultiSystem in the Strategy runtime, the parent account uses the BrokerTM synchronized with
9 * the broker, the sub-systems use their own shadow/virtual accounts (mode A/B is decided internally
10 * by MultiSystem).
11 *
12 * Invocation model: RunMultiSystemInStrategy::run() is called exactly ONCE per trading day at a
13 * fixed scheduled point (see crtMultiSysStrategy: 14:50 for daily or bar-interval seconds for
14 * intraday). The sub-systems replay the historical bars through their own virtual TradeManagers to
15 * generate signals
16 * ("backtest"); then at the final point (today's bar), the parent BrokerTM has already been
17 * synchronized from the broker snapshot. If the aggregated suggestions indicate a trade, the AF
18 * layer uses the parent getFunds(bar_datetime) to revalue the snapshot holdings at the queried
19 * bar's market price, corrects the order volume according to the current total assets, and places
20 * orders through the broker.
21 *
22 * Mode C (the shared real account of the legacy Portfolio) needs no extra live path: its
23 * sub-systems place their orders ON the parent BrokerTM itself, so those trades reach the broker
24 * exactly like the orders of the parent do, and the dual pools of the mode (the running pool and
25 * the force-sell pool) survive from one trading day to the next because the daily replay runs with
26 * reset=false. The broker-snapshot revaluation described below is what the AF conversions of modes
27 * A/B use; mode C does no conversion and places no second order, so it must not be "optimized"
28 * into the A/B pipeline.
29 *
30 * Critical design contract (prevent misread):
31 * - BrokerTM only stores cash + position quantities at the snapshot fetch time; their MARKET VALUE
32 * drifts with price as time flows. getFunds(datetime, ktype) MUST revalue the snapshot positions at
33 * the queried datetime (cash stays from the snapshot). Do NOT simplify it to always return the
34 * no-arg snapshot value (which uses m_datetime / lastDatetime, losing the time-precision). Do NOT
35 * change AF callers to no-date getFunds() either -- AF legitimately needs the bar-time valuation to
36 * size quotas and target shares.
37 * - There is NO zero-guard for dates earlier than the snapshot: getFunds(date) revalues the
38 * snapshot positions at ANY queried datetime using that moment's market price. Do NOT re-introduce
39 * a (datetime >= m_datetime) zero guard -- it broke the bar-time valuation, because the daily bar
40 * time (00:00) is always earlier than the snapshot time carrying intraday seconds.
41 */
