Trade cost algorithm for the Shanghai, Shenzhen and Beijing A-share at the current rates; it is the rolling default and always reflects the latest regime 

  1) transfer fee: since 2022-04-29, 0.01 per mille of the turnover amount, charged in both
     directions on all markets
  2) stamp duty: since 2023-08-28, 0.5 per mille, charged on sells only
  3) commission: charged in both directions, 5 yuan minimum
When the fees are cut again, update this model's defaults; the year-named models
(TC_FixedAPre2015 / 2015 / 2017) stay frozen for historical backtests.
参数
commissioncommission ratio, 1.8 per mille by default, i.e. 0.0018
lowestCommissionminimum commission value, 5 yuan by default
stamptaxstamp duty, 0.5 per mille by default, i.e. 0.0005
transferfeetransfer fee, 0.01 per mille of turnover by default, i.e. 0.00001

Trade cost algorithm for the Shanghai and Shenzhen A-share after August 1, 2015; it calculates the cost of every buy or sell 

The calculation rules are:
  1) Shanghai Stock Exchange
     Buy: commission + transfer fee
     Sell: commission + transfer fee + stamp duty
  2) Shenzhen Stock Exchange:
     Buy: commission
     Sell: commission + stamp duty
  Where: the current commission ratio is 1.8 per mille (5 yuan minimum), and the stamp duty is
  1 per mille
       After 2015 the transfer fee of the Shanghai Stock Exchange is 0.00002 of the turnover
amount
参数
commissioncommission ratio, 1.8 per mille by default, i.e. 0.0018
lowestCommissionminimum commission value, 5 yuan by default
stamptaxstamp duty, 1 per mille by default, i.e. 0.001
transferfeetransfer fee, 0.02 per mille by default, i.e. 0.00002

shares, minimum 1 yuan); the Shenzhen market does not charge the transfer fee before 2017.
