◆ AF_FixedAmount()

AFPtr HKU_API hku::AF_FixedAmount ( double amount = 20000.0)

#include </Users/fasiondog/workspace/hikyuu/hikyuu_cpp/hikyuu/trade_sys/allocatefunds/crt/AF_FixedAmount.h>

Fixed amount allocation, the trade of every selected asset cannot exceed this amount

L1 equal weight + L2 reads the parameter fixed_amount (mode A: a fixed amount per instrument / mode B: a fixed quota per sub-system). There are boundary differences with master FixedAmountFunds: master asserts amount>500 and stops when the remaining cash is less than 0.6×amount, while the new system uses >=0 and rebalances by the net amount.

参数
amountthe given fixed trade amount
返回
