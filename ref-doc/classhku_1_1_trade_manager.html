详细描述

Trade management module of the backtest simulated account; it manages the trade records and the fund usage of the account 

Default parameters:
precision(int): 2 calculation precision
support_borrow_cash(bool): false whether cash is borrowed automatically on a buy operation
support_borrow_stock(bool): false whether the stock is borrowed automatically when short selling

overridevirtual

Get all the daily asset records of the given date list

注解
The dates are processed in ascending order internally and the output keeps the input order; the dates taking the current-state branch are calculated serially first (they may advance the ex-rights data), then the historical dates are replayed from the trade list in one pass, O(trades + dates x positions)
参数
datesdate list
ktypeK-line type, it must match the date list, KQuery::DAY by default
返回
daily asset record list
