16/*
17 * Alexander Elder's safe zone stop-loss
18 * See "Come Into My Trading Room" (2007, Earthquake Press) by Alexander Elder, P202
19 * Calculation description: within the lookback period (generally 10 to 20 days), add up the lengths
20 * of all the downward crossings and divide by the number of the downward crossings to get
21 * the average noise, and subtract (the previous day's average noise multiplied by a
22 * multiple) from today's low price to get the stop-loss line. To offset the fluctuation and
23 * guarantee that the stop-loss line moves upward, the highest value within N days
24 * (generally 3 days) is taken based on the above result Note: the first (lookback period width +
25 * the width for taking the highest value) points in the returned result are invalid Parameters: n1:
26 * the lookback time window for calculating the average noise, 10 days by default n2: take the
27 * highest value within n2 days for the preliminary stop-loss line, 3 by default p: the noise
28 * coefficient, 2 by default
29 */
